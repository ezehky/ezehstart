# Trademark policy

The Ezeh Start **code** is free to use under the [MIT License](LICENSE). The **name** isn't
covered by that licence. This policy explains when you can use the name without asking,
and when you need permission.

> **Short version.** Build anything you like with the kit, and say it's "built with Ezeh
> Start". Don't name your product, company, domain or service after it, and don't suggest
> the project endorses you unless it does.

Any name that begins with **"Ezeh"** and is used for software, a starter kit or a

## Uses that need no permission

You may use the marks without asking, provided that all of the following are true:

1. your use follows this policy;
2. it's non-commercial, or it's one of the commercial uses listed as allowed below;
3. it refers to the actual Ezeh Start software, unmodified or clearly described as
   modified;
4. it doesn't suggest that the owner approves, sponsors or is affiliated with you, your
   product or your service when that isn't true.

Allowed, including commercially:

- **Saying what you built on.** "Built with Ezeh Start", "Based on the Ezeh Start starter
  kit", or "Powered by Ezeh Start".
- **Selling what you built.** An application built from the kit can be sold, hosted or
  licensed under *your own* name.
- **Writing about it.** Articles, reviews, tutorials, talks and comparisons that refer to
  Ezeh Start by name, accurately.
- **Describing compatibility.** Descriptive names such as "Stripe billing for Ezeh Start".
  "Ezeh Start Billing" or "EzehStart Pro" need permission.

## Uses that need permission

You need a written trademark licence for any of the following:

- using a mark as, or as part of, the name of a product, application, fork, distribution,
  theme, plugin or company;
- commercial software built on or including Ezeh Start that's marketed under the Ezeh
  Start name;
- training, courses, certification, conferences, consulting, hosting, support or design
  services offered *as* Ezeh Start services;
- domain names, social handles, URLs or app-store listings containing a mark;
- the logo, anywhere other than an unmodified reference to this project;
- merchandise bearing a mark;
- any service related to one of the uses above.

## Forks and modified versions

Fork freely, since that's what the MIT License is for. If you publish a fork, give it a
different name: remove the Ezeh Start name and logo from its branding, its `composer.json`
`name`, its default `APP_NAME` and its public pages. You may say it's "a fork of Ezeh
Start", as long as the fork's own name is clearly different and more prominent. Keep the
MIT copyright notice.

## How to use the marks

- Write the name as **Ezeh Start**: two words, both capitalised. Use `ezehstart` only for
  code identifiers and package names.
- Don't change the logo's proportions, colours or lettering, and don't combine it with
  other marks.
- Where it's practical, include: *"Ezeh Start is a trademark of Kingsley Uchenna Ezeh."*
- Don't use the marks in a way that's misleading, disparaging, or linked to unlawful
  activity.

## Asking for permission

Email **kingsley.uchenna.ezeh@gmail.com** and describe what you have in mind. Permission
may come with a short agreement to keep a stated quality standard, and it can be
withdrawn if that standard isn't kept.
